# Project brief

Build a mobile-first e-commerce website for one real medical store in Peddapalli, Telangana. Customers should be able to search and browse medicines and healthcare products, see current availability including out-of-stock items, add available products to a cart, and submit a guest cash-on-delivery order request with their contact and address details.

The staff area should provide authenticated inventory and order management, including medicine price and quantity editing, active/out-of-stock states, delivery settings, and validated CSV/XLSX bulk updates. The source remains in the private `KarthikeyaPodicheti/Pedapalli-Medical` repository and the demonstration storefront is deployed with GitHub Pages.

Use the green-and-white visual direction from the supplied pharmacy reference. Keep the experience responsive, accessible, and especially comfortable on mobile. Use suitable stock photography and Phosphor icons throughout the interface. Never invent real store credentials, licences, delivery promises, customer counts, medical advice, or verified inventory. Keep real checkout disabled until the backend, store data, delivery rules, staff access, and pharmacist workflow have been configured and reviewed.
